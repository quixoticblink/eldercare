Activated on 2026-06-25 when the team pivoted from a broad companionship HMW to the caregiver-respite marketplace direction. What lives here now:

- The strategic pathway and roadmap toward the September SGLN deliverable.
- Decision entries — one file per material decision, with the alternatives rejected and why.
- Frameworks the team uses to reason about the design space (marketplace typology, control axes, etc.).
- Business-case scaffolding — quantifiable impact metrics NCSS asked us to build toward.
- Partnership strategy — how we align with NCSS, Vanguard, AIC.

## Current entries

- [[respite-marketplace-concept]] — the working solution direction: a curated, certified caregiver-respite marketplace with two versions (near-term managed marketplace; far-term data-labeling → humanoid supply overlay).
- [[marketplace-2x2]] — Abhishek's framework for positioning our platform along two axes (matching control × management depth). The 2×2 that grounded the "top-right, managed marketplace" call.

## Capture rule

When we make a decision — adopt a positioning, pick a partnership, reject an alternative — write it here. One entry per decision. Include what you rejected and why. If a decision is later reversed, don't overwrite; write a new entry that references the old one. The evolution IS the evidence for the *Leadership Journey* rubric line.

## Feeds the rubric

- *Quality of Thinking* — *clarity of strategic pathway and roadmap.* This folder is the primary substrate.
- *MVP / Prototype — feasibility, viability, desirability.* The strategy entries frame what the prototype has to prove.
- *Leadership Journey — evidence of learning, adaptation, resilience.* The reversal-tolerant pattern (new entry, don't overwrite) is what makes this substrate legible over six months.
