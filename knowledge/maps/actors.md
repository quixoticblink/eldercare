## The convening layer

The programmes that convene us and set the rubric.

- **SGLN TECH** — Singapore Leaders Network Technology track. Programme running the six-month sprint. Runs the cohort sessions, sets the September Demo Day rubric. Not an operational partner; a *learning frame.*
- **Himmat** — external facilitator/coach for the SGLN cohort. Requested the HMW + empathy map deliverables that structured our May–June work.

## The government / sector layer

Where policy is set, funding flows, and referrals get routed.

- **MOH (Ministry of Health)** — sets national eldercare policy. Owns [[../landscape/vanguard]] via **MOH Holdings**. Reorganised the sector into the [[../landscape/iccp-model]] 85 sub-regions.
- **[[../landscape/aic]]** (Agency for Integrated Care) — private company with statutory role for MOH. Administers grants, runs the Population Health Visit framework, holds sector-level demand data (MET waitlist, referral flows). *Next partnership meeting after Vanguard.*
- **[[../landscape/ncss]]** (National Council of Social Service) — social-sector counterpart to AIC. Convened the eldercare initiative we're inside. Wants a business case they can take back to corporates.
- **MSF** (Ministry of Social and Family Development) — parent ministry over NCSS's mandate. Under growing health-social integration pressure with MOH.
- **SGO** (Silver Generation Office, at AIC) — outreach arm; runs door-to-door visits to seniors 65+ and refers to AACs. Foundation for the "80% of at-risk seniors engaged" claim.

## The operator layer

Who runs the actual services.

- **[[../landscape/vanguard]]** — only Ministry-owned home-care operator. Runs 5 nursing homes, multiple SCCs and AACs, home care, assisted-living pilot with HDB/MND. - **[[../landscape/lee-ah-mooi-old-age-home]]** — private old-age home on Thomson Lane. Boundary case for the private long-tail of the nursing-home tier. Not a partner; a study site.

## How they relate to each other

**MOH → MOH Holdings → Vanguard.** Direct ownership.

**MOH → AIC.** MOH commissions the Agency for Integrated Care as its sector coordinator.

**AIC ↔ NCSS.** Cross-agency counterparts (health-sector vs social-sector), increasingly integrated under health-social-integration pressure.

**NCSS → SSAs (Care Corner, Caring SG, CAL).** NCSS funds and validates. SSAs deliver.

**Vanguard, Care Corner, other operators → sub-regions.** Under [[../landscape/iccp-model]], each operator is assigned to specific sub-regions rather than serving nationwide. Pasir Ris is Vanguard's sandbox.

**MOH ← Ong Ye Kung.** Minister explicitly named social connectedness as a health determinant — political air cover for what we're building.

**NCSS → SGLN Tech.** The route by which our sprint got convened. NCSS wants a business case; SGLN wants a leadership-development artifact.

**Us ↔ Vanguard ↔ NCSS ↔ AIC.** The partnership chain. Vanguard is our operational anchor; NCSS is the introducer and sector integrator; AIC is where the sector-level demand-data lives.

**Luce, Homage, Jagami ↔ everything above.** Adjacent commercial players; not part of the government-partnership chain but operating in the same market.
