The SGLN Demo Day rubric has four dimensions, 25 points each. The wiki's folder structure is shaped by them — deliberately, so that we can look at any folder and ask "what does this feed?" This map keeps that alignment visible.

## The rubric

| Rubric line | 25 pts each | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| **Leadership Journey** | 25 | Evidence of learning, adaptation, resilience over six months. Personal growth and team dynamics. |
| **Quality of Thinking** | 25 | Evidence-backed analysis, clarity of strategic pathway, intellectual honesty. |
| **MVP / Prototype** | 25 | Human centricity, feasibility, viability, desirability. A real testable thing. |
| **Presentation & Influence** | 25 | Clarity, storytelling, executive presence. Does it convince? |

## Which folders feed which rubric line

### Leadership Journey (25 pts)

Fed by **`journal/`** — the day-by-day record of how we thought, what we tried, what we learned. Six months of dated entries is the substrate. If this folder is thin or has narrative gaps, this rubric line is hard to defend.

Also touches:
- **`purpose.md`** — team origination and lens.
- **`reframing/`** — the record of framing shifts as evidence of adaptation.
- **`maps/timeline`** and **`maps/hmw-evolution`** — narrative aggregation for the pitch.

### Quality of Thinking (25 pts)

Fed by three folders in balance:

- **`problem/`** — problem-space framing (currently thin; mostly lives in [[../eldercare]] and topic entries).
- **`evidence/`** — receipts. Statistics, research, primary interview findings, source documents in `evidence/sources/`.
- **`landscape/`** — existing solutions, prior art, partner ecosystem, competitive intelligence. Answers "what's been tried, what works, what doesn't."

Also touches:
- **`strategy/`** — the frameworks and decisions that make analysis actionable.
- **`reframing/`** — evidence-driven HMW iterations.

Judgment call for this line: is the analysis rigorous, is it honest about uncertainty, does it cite real sources, does it move confidently from evidence to conclusion?

### MVP / Prototype (25 pts)

Fed by:

- **`humans/`** — receipts for human centricity. Interviews, empathy maps, quotes, raw notes. The pitch's "we know who we're building for" evidence.
- **`prototype/`** — the actual working thing (still dormant as of 2026-07-11; wakes up ahead of the August 20 prototype milestone).
- **`strategy/respite-marketplace-concept`** — the pilot design that scopes what the prototype has to prove.

Also touches:
- **`images/`** — field-visit photos that anchor the human-centricity claim.
- **`landscape/vanguard`** and **`landscape/community-chaperone`** — the pilot context.

### Presentation & Influence (25 pts)

Fed by:

- **`pitch/`** — pitch drafts, narrative arc, slide content (dormant as of 2026-07-11; wakes up close to Demo Day).
- **`purpose.md`** — the opening frame most pitches will echo.

Draws material from every folder above; this is where the wiki collapses into a story.

## Highest-leverage gaps for the rubric

Reading the heat-map against the rubric:

**For Quality of Thinking:** the [[proposed-stubs]] with highest inbound-link count — `multi-payer-stack`, `crisis-activation-wedge`, `gig-vs-employee-supply-model` — deserve dedicated entries. Each is currently a phrase in [[../strategy/respite-marketplace-concept]] but referenced across the wiki.

**For MVP / Prototype:** `prototype/` needs to activate soon. The August 20 SGLN prototype milestone is coming; the folder should have design docs, wireframes, and — eventually — a runnable thing.

**For Presentation & Influence:** `pitch/` should activate around 2026-08 with narrative drafts, so we have time to iterate. Waiting until September is late.

**For Leadership Journey:** `journal/` needs to keep landing entries — every substantive meeting should produce one within a week. The map at [[timeline]] shouldn't have unexplained gaps.

## What "well-fed" looks like at Demo Day

If we were to run a mock-judging exercise today, the rubric would score roughly:

- **Leadership Journey:** strong (well-narrated journey with visible framing shifts).
- **Quality of Thinking:** strong-to-medium (evidence base is diverse but the multi-payer stack + crisis-activation-wedge write-ups need to land).
- **MVP / Prototype:** weak-currently (no prototype yet; entering the build window).
- **Presentation & Influence:** weak-currently (no pitch drafts yet).

The pattern is exactly what should be true two months in — the "how we got here" folders are thick, the "what we ship" folders are still empty. The build window is now.
