## May — origination

### 2026-05-19 · Day 1 · [[../journal/2026-05-19]]

First Zoom. Five of us. Family stories → negotiated convergence on companionship → six WHO/DO/OUTCOME rows → synthesised HMW: *"How might we help elderly in Singapore experience genuine daily connection…"* ([[../reframing/hmw-current]]).

Also this day: the roles that held through the sprint got assigned — Abhishek on GitHub + Vercel + wiki, Zheng Wei on interview questionnaire + statistics, Shobhit on cohort presentation format, Aditi on Himmat feedback + strategic critique, Lara on convening.

### 2026-05-19 → 2026-05-25 · Round 1 field interviews

Six interviews across four respondent types — a solution provider, a frontline caretaker (Lee Ah Mooi, [[../landscape/lee-ah-mooi-old-age-home]]), two children of elderly parents, two elderly people directly (one at Care Corner Toa Payoh 170, [[../landscape/care-corner]]).

Field photos at [[../images/README]].

### 2026-05-25 · Round 1 synthesis + evening working session

Two events on the same day. Interview synthesis: [[../journal/2026-05-25]]. Team working session that evening on the microsite workflow and empathy map: [[../journal/2026-05-25-team-sync]].

Six findings emerged, most consequentially:
- **Loneliness is an empty calendar** — the loss of structure, not just people.
- **AI companionship has a half-life** — the founder who builds AI companion products told us it "cannot be AI." This is the round's most disruptive finding.
- **Consistency is the actual product** — transient help fails.

The round-1 synthesis is at [[../evidence/interview-round-1-findings]].

## June — reframe

NCSS shared their year-old HMW — *"How might we enable caregivers to conveniently access respite in a timely manner?"* — and Aditi conceded on-camera that their framing was sharper than ours. Vanguard's operational pilots ([[../landscape/community-chaperone]]) were revealed. The ownership question ("who owns this in October?") was opened.

### 2026-06-25 · Solutioning session · [[../journal/2026-06-25-solutioning]]

The reframe *landed*. HMW pivoted from companionship to **caregiver respite** ([[../reframing/hmw-2026-06-25-caregiver-respite]]). Abhishek's [[../strategy/marketplace-2x2]] framework put the whiteboard debate on rails. Zheng Wei's blue-sky data-labeling-→-humanoid pipeline collapsed the marketplace-vs-robotics debate. The `strategy/` folder activated: [[../strategy/respite-marketplace-concept]].

## July — validation

### 2026-07-07 · Vanguard-prep session · [[../journal/2026-07-07-vanguard-prep]]

Lara's *skin-in-the-game* reframe changed the shape of the Vanguard ask: don't accept partner enthusiasm as evidence; convert it into commitment. Zheng Wei's fungibility question ("can supply move across ecosystems?") named a marketplace-theory point that hadn't surfaced. Entity/counterparty question opened.

### 2026-07-08 · Vanguard site visit · [[../journal/2026-07-08-vanguard-visit]]

The pivotal external validation moment. Vanguard walked us through their ICCP-model operational picture ([[../landscape/iccp-model]], [[../landscape/vanguard]], [[../landscape/singapore-care-taxonomy]]) and shared operational data ([[../evidence/vanguard-operational-data]]) — including the whiteboard demand extrapolation (1M seniors → 30% non-robust → 15–30K national bookings/month).

The wedge sharpened. Chaperone is small (20–30/month across 4 centres); the real pain is *crisis activation* — helper runaway, spousal-couple hospitalisation, discharge scramble. Angeline volunteered a Pasir Ris pilot by year-end. HMW narrowed again: [[../reframing/hmw-2026-07-08-post-vanguard]].

## September — Demo Day

### 2026-09-XX · SGLN Demo Day

The rubric event. Four dimensions × 25 pts = 100:

- **Leadership Journey** — evidence of learning, adaptation, resilience over six months. Fed primarily by `journal/`.
- **Quality of Thinking** — evidence-backed analysis, strategic clarity. Fed by `problem/`, `evidence/`, `landscape/`, `strategy/`.
- **MVP / Prototype** — human centricity, feasibility, viability, desirability. Fed by `humans/` + `prototype/`.
- **Presentation & Influence** — clarity, storytelling, executive presence. Fed by `pitch/`.

## The story in three sentences

- We started with an intuition about elderly companionship, but our own six field interviews and NCSS's IDIs both said the wedge was somewhere else.
- Vanguard walked us the rest of the way to *crisis-activation respite for caregivers*, in a Pasir Ris ICCP pilot, with a multi-payer stack that private pay alone can't sustain.
- What we ship in September is that concept made concrete — pilot design, business case, and prototype — with the consistency-as-design-constraint respected throughout.
