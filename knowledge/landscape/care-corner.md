Care Corner is one of Singapore's larger Social Service Agencies (SSAs), and it's been showing up in our wiki since the NCSS conversation on 2026-06-15. NCSS's late-2025 IDIs went through Care Corner's network to reach seniors and caregivers. The Toa Payoh 170 Active Ageing Centre is where several of our round 1 interviews happened. And under the emerging [[iccp-model]], Care Corner is the kind of sub-region-native operator that a caregiver-respite marketplace has to work through.

We visited the Toa Payoh 170 site during round 1 fieldwork on 2026-05-24 — see the photos in [[../images/README]].

**Update 2026-08-21:** Care Corner hosted the first [[../journal/2026-08-21-tabletop-vanguard-ncss|Table Top Exercise]] of the Kakis app at its **Toa Payoh 261A** Active Ageing Centre (Care), with Vanguard and NCSS facilitating and eight AAC members as participants. Photos in [[../images/README]]. That makes Care Corner the first operator whose members have used the live app, which is not the same thing as the Pasir Ris ICCP population the HMW names.

## What Care Corner runs

Multi-service SSA covering:

- **Active Ageing Centres** — the community-based, upstream, preventive layer of Singapore's eldercare taxonomy ([[singapore-care-taxonomy]]). Toa Payoh 170 is the site we've been to.
- Community services, family services, youth services, and mental-wellness programmes across other populations.
- Registered as a charity; funded through a mix of MOH/MSF grants, philanthropic capital, and CSR partnerships.

## The Toa Payoh 170 AAC — what we saw

From the field visit ([[../images/README]] IMG_9536 to IMG_9539):

**Physical setup.** Ground-floor space in an HDB block, walking distance from the fitness corner and the surrounding neighbourhood infrastructure. Signage in English and Chinese ("关怀活跃乐龄站"). Interior is a large open-plan room with folding chairs, small kitchen area, notice boards, and a staff desk near the entrance.

**The activity calendar** (IMG_9538 photo — May 2026):
- Weekday mornings: Low Impact Aerobics, Chair Zumba (10–11am, Block 171 pavilion), Zumba Gold, K-POP Lite Exercise, Muscle Strengthening Chair Exercise, Calligraphy (9:30–11:30am).
- Weekday afternoons: Cognitive Games (self-directed), Crochet (2–3:30pm, for registered members), Qi Gong (1:30–3pm, TP170), Golden Years Melodies with Hand-Bell (2–3pm), Rummy O.
- Weekly: Community Health Post (NHG), Wednesdays 2–5pm — vital signs, healthy lifestyle guidance, care/support planning, community-resource connections, geriatric assessment, medication review.
- Special: Intergenerational Art & Craft workshops with NUS, Karaoke.
- Public holiday closures marked. Some days "Closing Early 5:00 PM."

Two blocks operate as one AAC pair (Block 158 + Block 170 activities interleaved).

**The messaging.** Notice boards visible in IMG_9539: *"Be patient. Be kind. Please respect our staff"* (Care Corner Seniors Services branding — a signal about the emotional labour of staff), Food from the Heart distribution notice (June suspended), NUS intergenerational workshop poster (26 May, 2–4pm, registration full).

## Why they matter to us

Three threads.

**They're the round-1 fieldwork site.** Both the Toa Payoh AAC interview ([[../humans/2026-05-25-elderly-gentleman-toa-payoh-aac]]) and the ambient observations that shaped the empathy map's "coping" pole came from here. The activity calendar (IMG_9538) is direct evidence of the *day-has-a-shape* pattern the empathy map calls out — it's not abstract; it's a laminated grid on the wall.

**They're inside NCSS's research pipeline.** NCSS's late-2025 IDIs (~20–30 respondents, Chinese/Malay/English) were delivered via Care Corner's participant pool. The consistency finding in [[../evidence/ncss-idi-findings]] — seniors *repel* new helpers — was drawn partly from Care Corner-linked interviews.

**They're the archetypal sub-region operator.** Under the [[iccp-model]] 85-sub-region reorganisation, Care Corner is exactly the kind of community-embedded SSA that inherits the operating layer. If our caregiver-respite marketplace ever scales beyond a Vanguard-run Pasir Ris pilot, Care Corner's Toa Payoh footprint is the shape of the second-node deployment.

## Where they fit in the map

- **NCSS**-validated SSA for the late-2025 IDIs (see [[ncss]]).
- **AAC operator** under MOH/[[iccp-model]] framework.
- **Vanguard-adjacent** — different operator, different sub-region, potentially fungible supply and demand under a unified platform (open question, per Zheng Wei's fungibility line in [[../journal/2026-07-07-vanguard-prep]]).
- **Named next-step from NCSS** — we're supposed to divide-and-conquer between AIC and Care Corner for the follow-on meetings.

## What we'd want to learn from them

If we get a Care Corner meeting (per the divide-and-conquer plan from [[../journal/2026-07-07-vanguard-prep]]):

- What's their AAC attendance profile? The Vanguard whiteboard said 15–25% of seniors in a catchment are reached by AACs — is Care Corner Toa Payoh in that band, higher, lower?
- What caregiver-respite adjacencies do they run today, if any? Care planning? Chaperone equivalents?
- What's their supply model — salaried staff, volunteers, micro-workers? Recruitment pipeline?
- Would they be willing to be a second-node deployment for a caregiver-respite marketplace, following a Vanguard Pasir Ris pilot?
- What's their read on the [[../evidence/ncss-idi-findings]] consistency insight — does it match their day-to-day experience?
- How's their relationship with MOH and NCSS on data-sharing? What can they release aggregated?

## What confused us / open questions

- The intersection with NCSS's other validated SSAs — Caring SG, Caregiver Alliance (CAL) — is unclear. Do they cover different geographies, different populations, or overlapping?
- Is the Toa Payoh 170 AAC a representative Care Corner AAC, or an exceptionally-well-run one? Doesn't matter for the empathy work but matters for extrapolation.
