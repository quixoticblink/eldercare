Vanguard's Community Chaperone pilot is the closest existing operational thing to the marketplace we're designing. It started in April–May 2025 when Vanguard noticed that Medical Escort Transport (MET) — the subsidised programme for accompanied medical trips — covers medical trips but not *social* trips: haircuts, meals, grocery runs, family visits, or non-clinical outings where the senior needs supervision. Someone at Vanguard decided to plug the gap.

For our HMW ([[../reframing/hmw-2026-07-08-post-vanguard]]) the chaperone pilot is important not because chaperone is the wedge — the 2026-07-08 meeting made clear the volume is small and it's *not* where the biggest pain sits — but because it's the operational lab where every question we care about (matching, certification, pricing, supply) is already being answered by trial.

## What it is

An on-demand escort service. A certified staff member or micro-worker accompanies a senior to a bounded life activity the family caregiver can't cover in the moment:

- Clinic appointments (non-MET-eligible)
- Haircuts
- Meals out
- Grocery runs
- Family visits (including harder cases like visiting a relative in prison)
- Discharge escorts from hospital
- Social outings

The framing NCSS made explicit at the 2026-06-15 meeting still applies: *"it's not giving respite to the caregiver, it's giving access to respite to the caregiver."*

## The numbers, as of 2026-07-08

- **20–30 chaperone appointments per month**, across 4 senior-care centres.
- **160 clients served daily** across those 4 centres.
- Estimated **5–10% attach rate** for chaperone service among Vanguard's active client base in Pasir Ris.
- **Rejection rate: 3–4 out of every 20 requests** turned away, due to supply constraints.
- Requests typically need **3 days advance notice**.
- Vanguard is turning down referrals from St. Luke's Hospital because they can't take more.

## Supply

- **3–4 trained Vanguard staff per centre** (salaried, mainstream service track).
- **19–20 senior micro-workers per centre** — well/robust seniors in their early 60s, early retirees. Paid $10–12/hour. Ad hoc; no fixed commitment.
- **First formal micro-worker onboarding cycle starts July 2026.** Currently pilot scale.
- Not actively mass-recruiting.

## Pricing tension

- Vanguard pays micro-workers **$10–12/hour** — below the $20–22 comparable gig-market rate.
- Vanguard charges clients **$25–35/hour**.
- Cost basis per session including overhead is **~$26**.
- Many clients cannot pay $25–35 — Vanguard subsidises via internal healthcare fund and philanthropic capital.
- Michelle's honest read at the meeting: *"not so many of them would want to pay this amount for someone to bring them for social. But we did have a few cases where… we would use our own funds."*

## Operating model

- Cases come in **by phone**.
- A nurse does **manual phone triage** to assess complexity.
- **First session is always a Vanguard staff member** — micro-workers only take repeat sessions after the case is characterised.
- Subsequent visits: **manual roster / WhatsApp check** to assign a micro-worker.
- No formal software. No matching algorithm. No preference ranking (MOH/AIC hesitancy).

## Training the chaperone workforce

- **CPR (VCRF EAD)** — externally certified, done at St. Luke's Hospital. Mandatory.
- **Community Assistance and Conserve Bandwidth modules** — externally certified.
- **Mobility assistance** — in-house half-day training.
- **Dementia basics** — in-house module for the micro-workers who might handle dementia-related cases. Tiered readiness: only those who complete this module get deployed on dementia tasks.
- **Emergency management, SOPs, on-the-job training** — in-house.
- Training is **free of cost** to micro-workers.

## The consistency answer, partially

The [[../evidence/ncss-idi-findings]] consistency finding said seniors need months to adjust to a new helper and *repel* new faces at first contact. Vanguard's phone-triage → first-session-with-staff → subsequent-with-micro-worker pattern is a partial operational answer:

- The first face the senior sees is Vanguard staff, not a stranger from a marketplace pool.
- Once the case is characterised, matching to a specific micro-worker becomes a repeat-relationship pattern, not a lottery.

Whether this pattern scales to 2,000 monthly appointments is the open question. It clearly works at 20–30.

## The "consistency vs on-demand" tension inside the pilot

The three-days-advance-notice reality contradicts the on-demand branding. In practice, Community Chaperone as run today is *scheduled with short lead time*, not truly on-demand. The genuinely on-demand version (helper runaway, discharge scramble) is the crisis-activation category the [[../reframing/hmw-2026-07-08-post-vanguard]] targets — which is a different product than what Vanguard is running now.

## Why this pilot alone won't scale

- **Supply is thin.** Turning down 3–4 in 20 means demand exceeds capacity but demand growth exposes capacity gap.
- **Willingness-to-pay is broken.** $25–35 exceeds what many clients will pay. Multi-payer stack required.
- **Manual operations don't scale linearly.** Phone triage + WhatsApp roster works for 20–30/month per centre. Not for 200/month.
- **Referrals from St. Luke's are being *turned down.*** That's demand Vanguard sees but can't serve — a signal of the actual gap size.
- **No marketing.** The pilot is invisible outside Vanguard's own centre populations. This is why the [[../evidence/ncss-idi-findings]] "50%+ unaware of services" statistic matters.

## What Vanguard wants to do next

Angeline explicitly volunteered to pilot a broader service package at Pasir Ris **by year-end 2026**, layered on top of the existing ICCP platform. The package she named:

- **Caregiver planning** — the missing add-on when seniors register for home care.
- **Direct respite services** — chaperone, night respite, custodial drop-in.
- **Training** — caregiver skill-building.
- **Recreation** — hotel-corporate partnerships for low-season respite stays.

This is the shape of the pilot we should be designing into. See [[../strategy/respite-marketplace-concept]].
