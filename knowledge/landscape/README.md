What's already been tried in this space. One entry per company, initiative, product, or failed experiment.

For the rubric: *Quality of Thinking* rewards relevance to Singapore's tech ecosystem and clarity of strategic pathway. Knowing what's been tried, why it failed, and what's running — that's the substrate. Don't propose anything without checking this folder first.

## Stubs to fill in (from May 19 discussion)

- [[memory-lane-reminiscence-therapy]] — startup using photo and memory sharing for elderly mental wellness. Reminiscence therapy has scientific backing.
- [[oak-care-caregiver-support]] — startup addressing working-adult caregiver challenges (finding qualified domestic workers, training). Founded by someone who cared for a dying mother and sick grandmother.
- [[grab-voice-assistant]] — Grab's voice product for visually impaired and elderly users. 80k voice samples in local accents. Government-supported. Ministers reportedly very interested.
- [[ncss-grabcare-postmortem]] — failed on-demand companion-matching attempt for elderly needing hospital visits and daily activities. Legal concerns about stranger safety and verification. Existing advance-booking services had very low penetration. **Important reference** — this is the closest prior art to our problem space.
- [[hdb-community-networks]] — informal elderly support already happening at HDB block level. Often invisible to formal programs.
- [[silver-zones-singapore]] — traffic safety zones for elderly. Exist, but don't cover public transport.
- [[singpass-and-digital-access-grants]] — government grants to tech companies for elderly-friendly features (larger fonts, simplified flows).

## Capture rule

When you hear about a company, product, or initiative that touches eldercare, drop in a stub here with: what they do, when they launched, what's worked, what's failed, who runs it, and (if known) why. If the entry is for a postmortem, document the *specific reasons* it failed — generic "didn't get traction" is not useful.
