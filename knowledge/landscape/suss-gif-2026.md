The **Singapore University of Social Sciences (SUSS) Geronpreneurship Innovation Festival 2026** is an exhibition and industry-networking event on 26 August 2026 at Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre. It's directly on-theme for what we're building — the whole event is about *"solutions, services, and initiatives that support healthy longevity and purposeful ageing"* — and its date sits neatly between our Vanguard pilot conversations and our September SGLN Demo Day. Worth capturing as both a *potential exhibition venue for the September deliverable* and as *market-intelligence-and-partnership territory* regardless of whether we exhibit.

Theme for 2026: *"The Power of Purpose in Ageing: Our Future Chapter."* Organiser: SUSS. Visionary partner logo visible in the promotional material but not clearly identifiable from the screenshot.

## The event, in facts

- **Date:** 26 August 2026, 10am–6pm. Registration opens 9am.
- **Location:** Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre, Halls 403 & 404.
- **Format:** Exhibition + networking. Exhibitors set up booths; visitors are care organisations, longevity communities, active-ager communities, potential partners.
- **Contact:** gif@suss.edu.sg.
- **Early-bird rates for exhibitors:** extended to 15 July 2026 (per the marketing screenshot the user shared).
- **Value props for exhibiting:** showcase ideas and innovations to diverse stakeholders; raise awareness of impact; connect with potential partners in the ageing ecosystem; exchange insights with like-minded organisations.

## Why it's on our radar

**Timing.** August 26 is roughly four weeks before our September SGLN Demo Day. If we exhibit at GIF, whatever we show has to be *ready to demo to real ageing-sector stakeholders* by that date — not September. That's a forcing function on the September prototype design that could work for us or against us.

**Audience.** Care organisations, longevity communities, active-ager cohorts. That is *exactly* the stakeholder mix we need for partnership conversations post-Vanguard. Even attending without exhibiting is a real-world networking hit-list.

**Complementary to the SGLN sprint.** GIF is orthogonal to the SGLN masterclass series — different audience, different framing. Exhibiting here doesn't conflict with what SGLN wants from us and would be a defensible additional deliverable for the *"clarity of strategic pathway and roadmap"* rubric line.

**Cost is unknown but early-bird gate suggests $$$.** Any exhibition booth involves booth fee, materials, staffing. If our team of five has to fund it out of pocket that's a hurdle; if SGLN or a partner sponsors it, less so.

## Where SUSS sits in Singapore's eldercare ecosystem

- **SUSS itself** is an autonomous university with a strong focus on social sciences and lifelong learning. Its Geronpreneurship programme is a rare Singapore initiative that treats ageing as an *entrepreneurial* domain rather than a policy or clinical one.
- **Not one of the operators** we've been meeting (Vanguard, NCSS, AIC, Care Corner). More of a *knowledge / research / educational* node in the ecosystem.
- **Adjacent to National Silver Academy** — the government's lifelong-learning-for-seniors programme. SUSS is one of the delivery partners.

## Options for engagement — three tiers

**Tier 1: Exhibit.** Book a booth, present the concept, demo whatever we've built. Highest visibility, highest cost, highest forcing function on the timeline.

**Tier 2: Attend as observers.** No booth, just team members walking the floor. Cheap, useful for intelligence, mediocre for visibility.

**Tier 3: Present or panel.** Some conferences have a call-for-speakers alongside the exhibition. Worth checking if SUSS has a talks track and whether "How we built a caregiver-respite marketplace in six months as an SGLN TECH team" is a fit.

Not mutually exclusive — Tier 1 usually implies Tier 2, and Tier 3 could layer on top of either.

## What we'd need to decide

- **Are we ready to demo to stakeholders on 26 August?** The prototype timeline is currently anchored on the September Demo Day. Advancing it by four weeks is a real cost.
- **Who staffs the booth?** All five members probably can't take a Tuesday off; a rotating pair might work.
- **What does the pitch look like at GIF vs at the September Demo Day?** Different audience. GIF is stakeholder-and-partnership; Demo Day is judge-and-rubric. The narrative and materials would need to differ.
- **Does SGLN sanction / support this?** Worth confirming with Himmat or programme leadership before committing.
