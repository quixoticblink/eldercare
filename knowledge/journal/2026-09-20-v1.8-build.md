## The arc of the evening

- Synthesised the register into two lists: what can be fixed without a schema decision,
  and what cannot. Ten small items and ten large ones. Built the ten small ones.
- The bug first. A companionship kaki had been sent "Household help for Madam G". The
  roster already knew she didn't offer it and said so in grey. Now the row says
  "⚠ does not offer Household help", the confirm dialog says it again in capitals, and
  saying yes is a different audit action. Automatic matching never says yes.
- Then the things a befriender would notice on the next visit: urgent bookings of three
  to six hours, the kaki's message reading like a message, the family's note riding
  along with it, one door for availability, two gender chips, the care plan skippable,
  the cost hidden until asked for, plainer words on the way in.
- A review pass, nine findings, all small, all fixed. Backup, sync, two restarts, a check
  of the live sign-in screen and the help bot.

## What we learned

- **Grey text is not a warning.** The v1.6 roster showed "service not on profile" in the
  meta line under each kaki. The coordinator on 11 Sept read past it. The fix is the same
  information three times, in the row, in the dialog, and in the audit log, with the
  last one being the one that matters when someone asks later.
- **"Plain" and "polished" are about different readers.** The befriender who called the
  app too atas was reading screens; the request for more atas wording was about the SMS
  a kaki gets. Screens got shorter; the message got warmer. Both went out.
- **Rate files belong to the coordinator.** `assumptions.json` carries the notes we
  rewrote and the rates they edit. Overwriting it would have been a silent revert of
  their work; patching four keys on the box kept both.
- **The tests encoded the old words.** Seven specs failed on the first run because they
  asserted copy we had just changed. That is the cost of asserting sentences, and it is
  worth paying: every one of those failures was a screen we had changed on purpose.
