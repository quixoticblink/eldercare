The most boring folder in the wiki and the most important one. This is where the arc of the sprint gets recorded, one working session at a time. Six months from now, the rubric line *"evidence of learning, adaptation & resilience over 6 months"* is scored from here.

## How to add an entry

End of every working session, open a new file `YYYY-MM-DD.md` in this folder and write:

- The arc of the day (a few bullets — what happened, in order)
- What we learned
- What changed
- What's next

Keep it short. Five bullets per section is enough. The point is the accumulation, not the depth of any single entry.

Retros go in the same folder with the suffix `-retro.md` (e.g., `2026-06-15-retro.md`).
