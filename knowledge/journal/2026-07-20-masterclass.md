The cohort checkpoint. We walked the Padlet honestly ("~20 ideas, converged early"), showed the reframed HMW and the Kakis concept, and ran the speed carousel — groups rotating past our board, post-its and conversation. Full feedback synthesis: [[../evidence/sgln-masterclass-feedback-2026-07-20]].

## The arc of the day

- Presented the two-pivot journey (companionship → caregiver respite → need-arises activation) and the Vanguard/Pasir Ris anchor; the reframe and the research-first framing both landed well with the cohort.
- Micro-credentials were independently picked out as the differentiator — the certification-is-the-moat thesis survived external contact.
- A facilitator delivered the sharpest critique of the sprint so far: stop validating with partners, put the prototype in caregivers' hands — "give it to them, don't sell it to them." Now [[../reframing/devils-advocate]] critique 15.
- The cohort's questions clustered exactly on our open [ops] items: pricing, accreditation fees, abuse prevention, differentiation vs Homage/Luce — and someone named Kampung Kakis, which is also a name-collision warning for the working name.
- One concrete door opened: an offer to connect the pilot "under the smart cities network."

## What we learned

- External audiences independently converge on the same open questions we've logged with Vanguard — reassuring on completeness, damning on urgency.
- The safeguarding question (elderly who can't speak up against abuse) needs a first-class written answer, including why we would not adopt in-home surveillance.
- Our validation mix is lopsided: all supply-side since June. The demand side has never touched the product.

## What changed

- Aug 20 prototype milestone re-aimed: caregiver testing of 2–3 moments, not partner demos.
- Devil's advocate grew to 15 critiques.
- Naming decision accelerated (Kampung Kakis collision).
- New follow-up threads: Smart Cities Network contact; safeguarding entry; "considered and rejected" beat for the pitch.

## What's next

- Define the caregiver test plan (which 2–3 moments, which caregivers, given-not-pitched format) before August.
- Chase the Smart Cities Network offer while it's warm.
- Write [[safeguarding-and-abuse-prevention]] (proposed).
- AIC data meeting remains the top partnership priority.
