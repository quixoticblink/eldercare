## The arc of the session

Opened with logistics: Vanguard 9am tomorrow, in-person. July 20 masterclass in thirteen days — Padlet target 40 ideas, converge to 1–3 for prototyping, prototypes due August 20.

Padlet status: 15–16 ideas against a target of 40. Group agreed 40 is a box-check. Aditi's line: *"It's just checking a box. I don't know where they got the number 40."* Each person to add 4–5 more offline, but nobody's forcing it. Lara noted the *process* is going backwards — we've already converged on the marketplace idea and are back-filling ideation to satisfy the checkbox. Choose honesty over performance: in the July 20 sharing we'll say we maxed out at ~20.

Pivoted to Vanguard question-planning using the pptx (slide 5 = concept, slide 6 = questions). Shobhit anchored the two priorities: **size the market from Vanguard's view of use cases**, and **understand how supply gets managed** — certification, regulatory framework, current manual matching process, pool composition. Aditi added the meta-frame: what we need out of tomorrow is **feasibility validation + need validation.**

Zheng Wei raised a marketplace-mechanics question that hadn't come up before: **fungibility of supply across ecosystems.** Can a Vanguard-certified helper work outside Vanguard? Can a helper certified elsewhere plug into Vanguard? Without fungibility, this isn't a marketplace — it's a scheduling tool for one org.

Shobhit pushed on demand definition. Not just age 65+ — geographic concentration and GTM shape matter. If the problem statement is *"I need someone on short notice,"* multi-day manual matching won't work. What's the neighbourhood shape? AAC-anchored? Township-level?

Zheng Wei offered a strategic framing: back-engineer the case. If we can prove Vanguard has *underutilized certified supply* AND *external unmet demand* — the case writes itself.

**Then Lara delivered the sharpest reframe of the meeting.** Our partners (NCSS, Vanguard) don't have skin in the game. They're beneficiaries of whatever we build; we're the ones investing the time. So their optimism about the market is not evidence — they're rooting for us to succeed but they're not putting anything at risk. Zheng Wei and Aditi both concluded that for a *project* (not a real company), that over-excitement is acceptable and useful — cherry-pick the enthusiasm as feedback. Aditi then reframed it productively: **ask them what *they* will bring to the table** if we build this. Broadcast channels? Adoption commitment? Network access? That converts wishy-washy interest into actual commitment. This is now the fifth priority in the Vanguard question set.

Shobhit raised the counterparty question. A group of five can't sign an MOU. Can HCLI Tech be counterparty? Lara floated setting up our own NGO or Pte Ltd as a "day 2 problem" for the September presentation. Abhishek grounded it: NGO is much easier than for-profit — the for-profit path requires corporate approvals from four different employers (Grab, Thoughtworks, GoCheck, Accredify, IBM), which is likely to stall. NGO can be team-owned without those approvals. Deferred as a day-2 problem but on the radar now.

Lara to share the drive links. Aditi to export the Padlet (PDF, CSV, and markdown). Vanguard tomorrow will be voice-recorded (not live-noted). - **Padlet: 20+ ideas is enough.** We say so honestly in the July 20 sharing.

## What we learned

- Lara's *skin-in-the-game* framing changes the *shape* of the Vanguard ask. It's no longer "tell us what you see in the market" — it's "tell us what you'll commit." That's a strictly better question because partners' verbal enthusiasm is not information.
- The group publicly acknowledged the ideation process is now compliance theatre. Given we've converged, that's the honest read. Choosing to be honest about it in the July 20 sharing rather than perform 40 fake ideas.
- Zheng Wei's *fungibility of supply* question surfaced a marketplace-theory point we hadn't named. If supply doesn't move across ecosystems, we don't have a marketplace, we have a scheduling app for one partner. This is now a question with a real fork behind it.
- Shobhit's discipline holds: no single partner's market sizing is trustworthy. Vanguard's number tomorrow will be *one data point*, not *the answer*.
- Entity / counterparty is now a live question. It won't be solved tomorrow, but the September deliverable becomes very different if we're pitching an NGO idea vs. a study.

## What changed
