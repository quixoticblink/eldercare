Covers the first round of field interviews, conducted May 21–25, 2026.

## The arc of the round

- Ran six interviews over five days, reaching whoever we could reach quickly through a shared interview form.
- Spread across four respondent types: a solution provider (founder of a senior-tech company), a frontline caretaker (old age home), two children of elderly parents, and elderly people interviewed directly — including a field interview outside a Toa Payoh Active Ageing Centre and a visit to Lee Ah Mooi Old Age Home.
- Wrote up all six as conversation notes in [[../humans/README|humans/]].
- Synthesized them into six findings: [[../evidence/interview-round-1-findings]].

## What we learned

- Loneliness, defined by the people living it, is an empty calendar — the loss of structure, not just of people. This validates the "daily" in our HMW.
- The hardest finding: the solution provider who builds AI products for seniors told us AI companionship has a half-life — the feeling fades once users detect the patterns. "Cannot be AI."
- Connection runs through contribution (teaching, having a role), not just company. Being a recipient of care is not the same as feeling connected.
- Consistency is the actual product; transient and charity-shaped help fails. Family is the benchmark we keep losing to.

## What changed

- The implicit assumption that this gets solved with a technology companion is weaker than it was a week ago. Recorded as a sharpened critique in [[devils-advocate]].
- The WHO — "elderly in Singapore" — is now visibly too broad: the man coping fine in Toa Payoh and the woman eating alone in a full house are not one population.
- Team decision: record the HMW tension, don't rewrite the HMW unilaterally. The next HMW iteration engages it.

## What's next

- Build the empathy maps (May 26 deliverable) from the round 1 notes — expect the WHO to split into a "coping" persona and a "struggling / pride-gated" persona.
- Round 2 interviews: a fuller frontline-worker conversation, more elderly living alone, and an attempt to reach the pride-gated group via someone they already trust.
