## Current sources

- **`Singapore_Elderly_Loneliness_Research.pdf`** — the May 2026 Research Briefing that seeded [[singapore-eldery-stats-2]]. 18 pages covering Singapore demographics, global elderly population, loneliness definitions and prevalence (Hawkley & Cacioppo, Duke-NUS THE SIGNS Study, meta-analyses), health effects (Holt-Lunstad 2015, National Academies dementia meta-analysis, Duke-NUS 2021 life-expectancy findings), Singapore government policy inventory (Age Well SG, SGO, AAC 2.0, Silver Guardian, Healthier SG), and Singapore-specific causes (the "unneeded" identity from Maulod 2023, filial piety erosion, digital exclusion). Primary sources cited: SingStat Population Trends 2025; UN WPP 2024; Duke-NUS Malhotra et al. (JAGS 2021); Holt-Lunstad et al. (2015); Maulod, Ravindran & Chan (Innovation in Aging, 2023); National Academies (2020); NUS/Duke-NUS Gerontology (2021).

## Convention

When a new source is added:
- Copy the file here.
- Update this README with a one-paragraph description including primary studies cited.
- Update the evidence entry that uses it with a bottom-of-entry provenance note pointing at the source.
- If the source has a public URL, note it in the description above so future readers can verify.

For interview transcripts and meeting recordings, use the pattern `<date>-<label>.<ext>`. For research documents and academic-source PDFs, preserve the original filename where possible.
