## Attribution honesty

Reading the transcript carefully, two of the synthesis calls were less cleanly-attributable than they might look:

The DO — *"experience genuine daily connection"* — appears verbatim in ZW-1. But when Aditi verbally endorsed it in the group review, she credited Shobhit: *"I liked... I think Shobhit also wrote that, experience genuine daily connection... I think that's a good way to frame it."* Either both Zheng Wei and Shobhit wrote similar phrasing (plausible — Shobhit's row talks about "connected with the society, meaningful part") or Aditi misattributed in the moment. Fair to treat this as a jointly-arrived-at framing, not a clean single-author phrase.

The OUTCOME — *"Singapore is brimming with elderly communities..."* — the specific "brimming" wording is Lara's. But the actual synthesis, out loud, was Shobhit's. He proposed mixing his own *"quality of aging, or age well"* framing with Lara's brimming phrase: *"the quality of aging, or age well... leading to a lot of what you mentioned... hey, Singapore is brimming with the communities which are happy... reduces healthcare costs, mortality."* Shobhit is the synthesizer here, not Lara alone.

## What we noticed across the six attempts

The WHO clustered into two camps. Three of us (SS, AK, ZW) narrowed by living or family situation. Two (the AA-attributed collaborative row, LY) kept it general. Lara's inclusive framing won for the HMW — the argument being that "lonely" is state-dependent and might arbitrarily exclude users who feel lonely one day and not the next. Two side-debates remained open: Shobhit staked a provisional 65+ line; Abhishek pushed for 70+ or 75+ (arguing many Singaporeans work past 65, so a companionship product bites harder at older ages). The specific age question got deferred to sizing data.

The DO clustered around connection but with subtly different mechanisms. *Feel less lonely* is the most negation-shaped. *Feel connected with society* is broad and outward-facing. *Be more connected with a family member* is structural and specific. *Genuine daily connection* and *meaningful companionship* are about relationship quality. *Sense of belonging / assurance not left behind* is internal and emotional. The team picked the one that names quality and rhythm together — while acknowledging Lara's on-record confusion about how to fill this cell honestly.

The OUTCOME clustered into three scales: individual flourishing (age happily, feel seen and alive), systemic outcomes (reduce healthcare burden, increase life expectancy), and societal vision (Singapore brimming with communities). **Aditi's healthcare-burden reframe** was the sharpest strategic intervention: she pushed back on Shobhit's "increase life expectancy" as an outcome, pointing out that from a government or investor lens, longer life in an already-aging society *is itself the pressure* — and that the right framing is *reducing healthcare burden* and *easing sandwich-generation caregiving load*. Lara reinforced it in chat: reframe from "how long" to "how well." The synthesis went to societal scale, but with that healthcare-burden logic underneath.
