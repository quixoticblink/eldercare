This is where the framing of the problem lives and gets challenged. The two main artifacts:

1. **HMW iterations** — `hmw-<date>-<slug>.md`. We keep old ones because the rubric line "evidence of learning, adaptation & resilience over 6 months" is partly evidenced by how the HMW evolved.
2. **Devil's advocate** — the strongest case AGAINST the current framing. Someone owns this for at least a sprint.

## How this folder grows

When the framing shifts — a conversation reframes the problem, a stat changes our priors, a devil's advocate argument lands — open a new dated HMW file (`hmw-YYYY-MM-DD-<short-slug>.md`), explain at the top what changed and why, and update `hmw-current.md` to point at it. Old HMWs stay in the folder because the journey IS the evidence.

When the devil's advocate finds something the HMW can't survive, that's a feature, not a bug. The HMW should change.
