## What these photos collectively evidence

The photos capture in a single visual the pattern that shows up as "consistency vs transience" across our entire wiki:

- The Care Corner AAC is *bustling with structured daily activity* — the calendar is dense, the branding fresh, the volunteer/staff engagement current. That's the "coping" pole of the empathy map.
- Lee Ah Mooi is *quiet and time-stopped* — CNY decorations from months ago still up, volunteer memorabilia from three-plus years back still on display as recent history. That's the "struggling" pole, made tangible.

Same city, ten kilometres apart. Same underlying population. Very different care experience.
